MY HEALTH · OPTIONAL

Notice your own wellbeing patterns without turning them into a diagnosis.

Wearable measurements can add physiological context, while your own check-in remains the source of truth for how you actually feel.

Wellness context, not mood diagnosis. A watch can observe sleep, heart-rate, activity and selected physiological patterns, but Swasthya must not claim those signals alone prove you are happy, sad, stressed, focused or unwell.
