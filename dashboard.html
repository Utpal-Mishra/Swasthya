MY HEALTH · OPTIONAL

Notice your own wellbeing patterns without turning them into a diagnosis.

This personal layer stays secondary to Swasthya’s main Around Me experience. Wearable measurements can add physiological context, while your own check-in remains the source of truth for how you actually feel.

Wellness context, not mood diagnosis. A watch can observe sleep, heart-rate and activity patterns, but Swasthya must not claim that those signals alone prove you are happy, sad, stressed, focused or unwell.

WEARABLE CONTEXT

Samsung Health + your own mood anchor

Android companion required

The GitHub Pages website cannot directly read Samsung Health. The production path is an opt-in Android companion using Samsung Health Data SDK or Health Connect. Swasthya will use wearable measurements as context around recovery, arousal and fatigue—not as a machine-generated emotional diagnosis.
