MY HEALTH · OPTIONAL

Notice your own wellbeing patterns without turning them into a diagnosis.

Wearable measurements can add recovery and physiological-arousal context, while your own check-in remains the source of truth for how you actually feel.

Wearable context, not emotion detection. A watch can observe sleep, heart-rate, HRV, activity and selected physiological patterns. Those signals can support stress/recovery analysis, but they do not reliably prove that you are happy, sad, focused, anxious or depressed.
